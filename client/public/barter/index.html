The Trade & Barter House

This is the room Michigan Money Saver was built for. No prices, no percentages, no money changing hands. You bring what you have, you say what you want, and you and a neighbor work out the trade yourselves. The house just keeps the ground — it never touches the goods and it never touches money, because there is none here.

Browsing is free forever — look all you want. To post what you have and propose trades, you join the house. One membership opens both wings.

Goods — trade what you have

Equipment, tools, stones, firewood, the thing in the barn somebody else needs. Post a HAVE, say what you'd take for it, trade it for the thing you actually want.

Enter the Goods Wing

Services — trade what you can do

Labor is the one thing every tradesman has extra of and every homeowner is short of. A website for a plumbing job. Firewood splitting for a weld. Your skill against the job you need done.

Meet in a public place. The house doesn't hold goods or money and can't get either back for you.

Trades count as income at fair value — ask your tax person. The house doesn't report your trades for you.

Nobody here earns anything for bringing in a member. Membership is ground rent, plain and simple — the only money the house ever takes is the rent itself.
